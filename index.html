CLASS 11 · PHYSICS

Units and Measurements

Learn physical quantities, SI units, dimensions and errors.

Read Sample Notes
  • Physical quantity: A measurable property.
  • SI unit of length: metre (m).
  • SI unit of mass: kilogram (kg).
  • SI unit of time: second (s).
  • Dimensional formula: Expresses a quantity in terms of fundamental dimensions.
CLASS 11 · CHEMISTRY

Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry

Revise the mole concept, molar mass and chemical calculations.

Read Sample Notes
  • One mole: Contains approximately 6.022 × 10²³ entities.
  • Avogadro constant: 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹.
  • Number of moles: Given mass ÷ molar mass.
  • Always use consistent units in calculations.
CLASS 11 · BIOLOGY

Cell: The Unit of Life

Explore cell structure and the functions of important organelles.

Read Sample Notes
  • Nucleus: Contains most of the cell's genetic material.
  • Mitochondria: Major site of aerobic respiration and ATP production.
  • Ribosomes: Sites of protein synthesis.
  • Cell membrane: Selectively regulates movement of substances.
CLASS 12 · PHYSICS

Electric Charges and Fields

Study electric charge, Coulomb's law and electric fields.

Read Sample Notes
  • Like charges repel; unlike charges attract.
  • Charge is conserved in an isolated system.
  • Coulomb's law: F = k|q₁q₂|/r².
  • The SI unit of charge is the coulomb (C).
CLASS 12 · CHEMISTRY

Solutions

Learn concentration terms and properties of solutions.

Read Sample Notes
  • Molarity: Moles of solute per litre of solution.
  • Formula: M = moles of solute / volume of solution in litres.
  • Temperature can affect volume and therefore molarity.
  • Use the correct units in every numerical problem.
CLASS 12 · BIOLOGY

Principles of Inheritance

Revise Mendelian genetics, alleles and inheritance patterns.

Read Sample Notes
  • Gene: A DNA sequence associated with a functional product.
  • Alleles: Alternative forms of a gene.
  • Genotype: Genetic constitution for a trait.
  • Phenotype: Observable characteristics of an organism.
